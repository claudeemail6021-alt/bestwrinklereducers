Skin in your 40s is fundamentally different from skin in your 20s or 30s. Collagen production has slowed significantly — you lose roughly 1% of your collagen per year after 20, and the pace accelerates after menopause. Cellular turnover slows. The skin barrier becomes less efficient. Fine lines deepen into wrinkles. Volume loss changes contours.

A routine that worked in your 30s may no longer be enough. And the sprawling multi-step routines popular on social media are largely unnecessary. What you need is a focused, evidence-based approach with the right ingredients at the right concentrations.

Here is the complete routine.

## Morning Routine

**Step 1: Gentle Cleanser**

Morning cleansing removes overnight product residue and preps skin for actives. A gentle, non-stripping cleanser preserves the skin barrier — which matters more in your 40s when the barrier is less resilient. Look for ceramide-containing or low-pH formulas.

**Step 2: Vitamin C Serum (optional but recommended)**

Vitamin C in the morning provides antioxidant protection against the UV and pollution damage that drives most visible aging. It also directly stimulates collagen synthesis, making it one of the most evidence-backed morning actives.

If your budget allows one addition to a basic routine, a stable Vitamin C serum (look for L-ascorbic acid or stable derivatives like ascorbyl glucoside) applied in the morning is a high-value addition.

**Step 3: Moisturizer**

In your 40s, a moisturizer is not optional — it is foundational. The skin barrier becomes less efficient at retaining moisture, and chronically dehydrated skin both looks older and ages faster.

**Step 4: SPF 30+ (non-negotiable)**

UV exposure is the single largest driver of visible skin aging — responsible for up to 90% of the visible changes we associate with aged skin. No anti-aging routine is complete without daily sun protection. Use SPF 30 as an absolute minimum; SPF 50 is preferable.

Apply it as your final morning step, every day, regardless of whether you plan to go outside.
