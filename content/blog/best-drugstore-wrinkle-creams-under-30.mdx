## What to Look for on Drugstore Labels

Shopping drugstore can be confusing because brands use marketing language to obscure (or exaggerate) active ingredient concentrations. Here's what to look for:

**For wrinkle reduction:**
- Retinol (listed early in the ingredients = higher concentration)
- Retinyl Propionate / Retinyl Palmitate (gentler retinol derivatives)
- Niacinamide 3–5%

**For firming:**
- Peptides (Palmitoyl Oligopeptide, Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7)
- Adenosine (FDA-recognized anti-wrinkle ingredient)

**For hydration that plumps wrinkles:**
- Hyaluronic Acid (multiple weights if possible)
- Glycerin
- Ceramides

## Ingredients to Ignore at Drugstore Prices

**Collagen in the formula:** Large collagen molecules can't penetrate skin — it's a marketing ingredient. Stick to peptides that stimulate your skin's *own* collagen production.

**"Stem cells":** Usually plant-derived extracts with minimal evidence at the concentrations used in sub-$30 formulas.

**Very long ingredient lists:** More ingredients doesn't mean better — it often means lower concentrations of the actives that actually work.
